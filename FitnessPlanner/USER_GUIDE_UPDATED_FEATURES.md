## 📱 How to Use

### Viewing Today's Results

**Step 1:** Open Daily Challenges
- From dashboard, click "Daily Challenges"

**Step 2:** Check Your Progress
- Look at "📋 Today's Results by Category" card
- See your progress for each category
- View points earned per category

**Step 3:** Complete Challenges
- Check off items as you complete them
- Watch results update in real-time!
- See your points increase instantly

### Navigating to History

**Step 1:** Click "📊 View History"
- Button is at the top of the page
- Next to "← Back to Dashboard"

**Step 2:** Explore History Tabs
- **📅 Current Week** - Last 7 days breakdown
- **📈 Last 4 Weeks** - Multi-week comparison
- **🏆 All Time Stats** - Lifetime achievements

**Step 3:** Return to Challenges
- Click "← Back to Challenges" button
- Your progress is saved and preserved!

## 📊 Understanding Your Results

### Category Progress Bars

**Color Coding:**
- 🟢 **Green (75-100%)** - Excellent! Keep it up!
- 🟡 **Yellow (50-74%)** - Good progress!
- 🟠 **Orange (25-49%)** - Keep going!
- 🔴 **Red (0-24%)** - Needs attention

### Points System

**Exercise (24 points max)**
- 6 points per item × 4 items
- Focus: Physical activity

**Nutrition (20 points max)**
- 4 points per item × 5 items
- Focus: Healthy eating

**Hydration (16 points max)**
- 4 points per item × 4 items
- Focus: Water intake

**Recovery (20 points max)**
- 5 points per item × 4 items
- Focus: Rest and sleep

**Daily Maximum: 80 points**

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why don't I see results in the daily tab?**
A: Start checking off challenges! Results appear once you have data.

**Q: Do results update automatically?**
A: Yes! Every checkbox change updates results instantly.

**Q: Can I see yesterday's results?**
A: Yes! Click "View History" → "Current Week" tab.

**Q: How do I get back to challenges from history?**
A: Click the "← Back to Challenges" button at the top.

**Q: Will my progress be lost when I switch views?**
A: No! Everything is saved automatically.

**Q: Can I edit past days?**
A: Currently, you can only track the current day. Past days are view-only.
