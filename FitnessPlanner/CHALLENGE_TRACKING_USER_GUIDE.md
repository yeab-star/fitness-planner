# Challenge Tracking - User Guide

## 🎯 How to Use the Challenge System

### Accessing Challenges
1. Log in to your fitness planner account
2. From the dashboard, click **"Daily Challenges"** or **"View Challenges"**
3. The Combined Challenge View will open

---

## 📱 Daily Challenges Tab

### What You'll See:
- **Today's Date** - Current date displayed at the top
- **Motivation Message** - Daily inspirational quote
- **Progress Bar** - Visual representation of your daily completion
- **Points Counter** - Shows points earned today
- **Four Challenge Categories:**
  - 🏋️ **Exercise** - Physical activity challenges
  - 🥗 **Nutrition** - Healthy eating goals
  - 💧 **Hydration** - Water intake targets
  - 😴 **Recovery** - Rest and sleep goals

### How to Track:
1. ✅ **Check the box** when you complete a challenge item
2. ⬜ **Uncheck** if you need to correct a mistake
3. **Progress updates automatically** as you check items
4. **Points are calculated** based on completed items
5. **Data is saved immediately** to the database

### Celebration Messages:
- 🎉 **100% Complete:** "Perfect Day! All challenges completed!"
- ⭐ **75%+ Complete:** "Excellent progress! Keep it up!"
- 👍 **50%+ Complete:** "Great job! You're halfway there!"

## 🎯 Points System

### How Points Work:
Each challenge category has different point values:

**Exercise (24 points max):**
- 6 points per item × 4 items

**Nutrition (20 points max):**
- 4 points per item × 5 items

**Hydration (16 points max):**
- 4 points per item × 4 items

**Recovery (20 points max):**
- 5 points per item × 4 items

**Daily Maximum:** 80 points (all challenges completed)

---

## 🔥 Streak Tracking

### What is a Streak?
A streak is consecutive days where you complete ≥75% of your challenges.

### Types of Streaks:
- **Current Streak** - Your ongoing consecutive days
- **Longest Streak** - Your best performance ever
- **Category Streaks** - Tracked separately for each challenge type

### Maintaining Your Streak:
- Complete at least 75% of daily challenges
- Check in every day
- Even partial completion counts toward some stats!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I edit past days?**
A: Currently, you can only track the current day. Historical data is view-only.

**Q: What happens if I miss a day?**
A: Your current streak resets, but your longest streak and all-time stats remain.

**Q: How are points calculated?**
A: Each completed challenge item earns points based on its category (see Points System above).

**Q: What counts as a "completed day"?**
A: Any day where you complete 75% or more of your challenges.

**Q: Can I customize challenge items?**
A: Challenge items are currently generated daily based on best practices. Customization may be added in future updates.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes! All data is stored locally in your database and is only accessible to you.
