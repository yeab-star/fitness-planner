## Troubleshooting

### Q: My checkboxes are unchecked!
**A:** Check the date - if it's a new day, you get fresh checkboxes. Yesterday's data is in History view.

### Q: I checked items but they're gone
**A:** This should not happen anymore! If it does:
1. Check you're looking at today's date
2. Try closing and reopening
3. Check History view for your data

### Q: Can I see yesterday's checkboxes?
**A:** Yes! Click "View History" → "Current Week" tab to see the last 7 days.

### Q: What if I accidentally uncheck something?
**A:** Just check it again! Changes save instantly.

### Q: Can I edit past days?
**A:** Currently, you can only track today. Past days are view-only in History.
